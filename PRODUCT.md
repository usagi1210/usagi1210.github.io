# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

PhD admission committees, research supervisors, and academic peers worldwide. They visit with a specific intent: assess this person's research potential, intellectual depth, and fit for graduate study. Secondary audience: long-term academic networking — researchers in computer vision and computational imaging who may collaborate, cite, or follow this person's work over years.

## Product Purpose

A personal academic homepage for Yuan Junhao, undergraduate student (junior) in Intelligent Science and Technology at a Chinese university, specializing in Computer Vision and Computational Imaging. The site serves as the primary digital identity for PhD applications and long-term academic presence. It must make a strong impression with limited formal credentials (one co-authored publication, national awards, GitHub projects in progress) by leading with intellectual character and research taste rather than volume of output.

## Brand Personality

Precise · Modern · Ambitious

Not the cautious, template-filling applicant. The person who already thinks like a researcher: sharp on details, has strong aesthetic sensibility, and is clearly going somewhere.

## Anti-references

- SaaS product pages: gradient CTAs, "seamless experience" copy, metric-hero blocks, floating cards with drop shadows
- Domestic Chinese university official websites: red-gold palette, table-based layouts, bureaucratic information density, no visual hierarchy
- Generic academic CV templates: Bootstrap-era themes, Times New Roman on white, no spatial rhythm

## Design Principles

1. **Lead with character, not credentials.** The research interests and intellectual voice should carry the first impression. Credentials (one paper, awards) follow naturally but don't anchor the page.
2. **Editorial restraint over decoration.** Every visual element must earn its place. Whitespace is structure, not emptiness.
3. **Typography does the heavy lifting.** Hierarchy through scale and weight contrast, not color or ornament. The page should feel designed even in monochrome.
4. **Information at the right altitude.** PhD committees want to scan fast. Papers, awards, and projects must be findable in under 10 seconds without visual noise obscuring them.
5. **Built to grow.** Currently sparse on credentials; the design must not feel thin. As publications and projects accumulate, the structure absorbs them gracefully.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text contrast ≥ 4.5:1. Reduced motion alternative for all animations. Semantic HTML throughout (no div soup). All links have meaningful text for screen readers.
